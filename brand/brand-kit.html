Logo

One mark, four approved versions

Use the three-tone mark wherever full colour is possible. On boxes, stamps and anything printed in a single colour, use the one-colour mark. The letters and gaps are cut-outs, so the surface underneath shows through them.

Three-tone

Website, visiting cards, letterhead, signboards.

Three-tone on dark

The website header, social posts, the WhatsApp display picture.

One colour on kraft

Flexo print or rubber stamp on brown corrugated board.

Reversed

White on a solid brown or dark panel, such as tape or a vehicle.

Spacing & size

Give it room, keep it legible

Clear space

Keep a quarter of the mark's width clear on all sides. Nothing else, such as text, edges or folds, goes inside that space.

Minimum size

Screen: no smaller than 24 px across. For the 16 px browser tab icon, use the favicon file and don't shrink the main logo.

Print: no smaller than 12 mm across on paper, and 20 mm on corrugated board. Flexo ink spreads on the flutes and fills in the cut-out letters below that size.

Colour

Kraft brown, in three shades

The three shades light the cube the way a real carton is lit: the top face is lightest and the right side is darkest. Kraft Brown is the single ink colour. When printing, match it to a physical kraft proof rather than to a screen.
