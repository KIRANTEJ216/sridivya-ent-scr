Photoreal documentary industrial photography inside one clean, modern corrugated packaging factory in Hyderabad, India. Warm late-afternoon light through high clerestory skylights, cool steel-blue machinery, natural kraft-brown paper and board, light haze in the air. Full-frame cinema camera, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, muted teal-and-amber grade. Main subject centred in the middle third of the frame, depth stacked front to back, calm darker negative space on the lower left for headline text. No visible faces, no text, no logos, no watermarks.
